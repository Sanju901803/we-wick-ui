📈 Ideal Investment Order of Operations
  1. Capture any employer RRSP/pension match first. An employer match is an instant, guaranteed 100% return — always contribute at least enough to get the full match before anything else.
  2. Max out your RRSP room next, especially in high-income years.   3. Then fill your TFSA. Annual TFSA room is {{ tfsaAnnualLimit | currency:'CAD':'symbol':'1.0-0' }} — growth and withdrawals are entirely tax-free, making it ideal for shorter-term goals or once RRSP room is used up.
  4. Time large RRSP contributions to your highest-income years. The tax deduction is worth more in years you're in a higher bracket — if income varies year to year, prioritize maxing RRSP in your highest-earning years and consider FHSA/non-registered investing in lower-income years.
